**HOOK (150-200 words, no header):**

Build from the fear narrative placeholder's register. The hook has three movements:

1. **Name the unease (paragraph 1):** Preserve and expand the "You've been thinking it" energy. Second-person, intimate. The reader is a quality professional carrying low-grade anxiety they haven't articulated. Do NOT reference specific AI capabilities here (test matrices, edge case enumeration). The hook's job is emotional, not informational.

2. **State the fear flat:** "AI is coming for QA jobs" or equivalent. No drama, no dancing around it. Stated plainly in the same manifesto register as the rest of the post.

3. **Reframe (paragraph 2):** Validate the fear first ("You have earned the right to hold that fear"). Then reframe the mechanism: the fear is wrong about what it points at. Not replacement. Irrelevance by default. Do NOT resolve the tension — do not name quality systems thinking, do not preview the examples. The hook creates the frame; examples carry the argument. Stop after stating the reframe.

Voice constraints: No em dashes. No hedging on claims. No passive voice. No "adapt or die" rhetoric. No tool names. Second-person throughout.

Essential phrase that must appear in the hook region: language conveying "the fear is wrong about the mechanism — not replacement, irrelevance by default."

**CLOSE (150-200 words, no header):**

Apply the three-beat close structure from the-same-blind-spot-twice.mdx. The close follows the synthesis section and lands the argument as a professional directive.

Three beats:
- Beat 1: Validate — the fear was real, the mechanism was wrong, the job is not gone
- Beat 2: Name — quality systems thinking is now the explicit job, not the implicit one. The reader must claim it deliberately.
- Beat 3: Challenge — not claiming this means the vacuum gets filled by default. Name the consequence of inaction without softening.

Final sharp image: The vacuum image from the design doc — "the quality professional who does not claim this role leaves a vacuum. AI fills vacuums. It fills them confidently and completely and often wrong."

Locked closing line (verbatim, non-negotiable, must be the final sentence): "This is the work now, and it's yours."

No sentence after the locked line. No softening. No comfort.

Voice constraints: Same as hook — no em dashes, no hedging, no passive voice, no tool names. Shift to "you" register for the directive.
